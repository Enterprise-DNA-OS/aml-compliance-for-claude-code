# The rules this register checks

`/compliance` runs every check below against the records and cites the rule with each finding. The numbers (deadlines, thresholds, cycles) are rows in the `rules` table, so a change in the law or in your programme is a one-line migration through `/customise`. Rows marked *your setting* are this system's defaults where the law leaves the frequency to your programme.

This is a records check. It is not legal advice, and it does not replace your AML/CFT or AML/CTF programme. If a rule here looks out of date, stop and confirm it with your supervisor's guidance before changing it.

## New Zealand: AML/CFT Act 2009, supervised by DIA

DIA became the single AML/CFT supervisor on 1 July 2026.

| Check | What a breach looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CDD-BEFORE-SERVICE | A matter opened before the customer's identity was verified | AML/CFT Act 2009 s16 |
| TRUST-EDD | A trust on simplified or standard CDD | s22(1)(a) |
| HIGH-RISK-EDD | A high risk customer not on enhanced CDD, or with no source of wealth or funds | s22 and s23 |
| PEP-EDD | A foreign PEP (or a foreign PEP owner) without enhanced CDD, senior manager approval and verified source of wealth | s26 |
| OWNERS-MISSING, OWNER-UNVERIFIED | A non-individual with no beneficial owners recorded; owners or trustees not verified | s15, s16 and s17 |
| REVIEW-OVERDUE | Ongoing CDD review past its date (12, 24 or 36 months by risk, *your setting*) | s31 |
| REPORT-LATE (SAR) | A suspicious activity report not lodged within 3 working days of forming the suspicion, or 5 for a law firm | s40, as amended by the Statutes Amendment Act 2025 (in force 27 November 2025) |
| REPORT-LATE (PTR) | A large cash transaction (NZ$10,000 or more) not reported within 20 working days | s48A, as amended by the Statutes Amendment Act 2025 |
| CO-MISSING | No AML/CFT compliance officer recorded | s56(2) |
| VETTING, TRAINING | Staff with AML duties not vetted, or not trained in the last 12 months (*your setting*) | s57(1)(a) and (b) |
| RISK-ASSESSMENT, PROGRAMME-REVIEW | None on file, or not reviewed in 12 months (*your setting*) | s57, s58 and s59(1) |
| AUDIT | Risk assessment and programme not audited in the last 3 years | s59(2); AML/CFT (Requirements and Compliance) Amendment Regulations 2021 (every 4 years if DIA says so) |
| ANNUAL-REPORT | The annual report for the year to 30 June not filed with DIA by 31 August | s60; DIA, Annual AML/CFT Report |
| SANCTIONS | A live customer with no sanctions screen on file | Terrorism Suppression Act 2002, United Nations Act 1946, Russia Sanctions Act 2022 |

Records are kept for at least 5 years after the relationship ends (s49 to s51). `/retention` shows the keep-until date for every ended file. Tipping off is prohibited (s46): customer letters drafted here ask for CDD information only.

## Australia: AML/CTF Act 2006 as amended in 2024, supervised by AUSTRAC

Tranche 2 businesses (lawyers, accountants, conveyancers, real estate, trust and company service providers) carry obligations from 1 July 2026.

| Check | What a breach looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CDD-BEFORE-SERVICE | A designated service started before initial CDD verified the customer | AML/CTF Act 2006 s28 |
| HIGH-RISK-EDD, PEP-EDD | High risk customers and foreign PEPs without enhanced CDD, senior management approval and source of wealth | AML/CTF Act 2006 as amended 2024; AUSTRAC reform guidance |
| OWNERS-MISSING, OWNER-UNVERIFIED | Beneficial owners not identified or not verified | AUSTRAC reform guidance |
| REVIEW-OVERDUE | Ongoing CDD review past its date (*your setting*) | AML/CTF Act 2006 as amended 2024: ongoing CDD |
| REPORT-LATE (SMR) | A suspicious matter report not lodged within 3 business days, or 24 hours for terrorism financing | s41; AUSTRAC, suspicious matter reports |
| REPORT-LATE (TTR) | Physical cash of A$10,000 or more not reported within 10 business days | s43; AUSTRAC, threshold transaction reports |
| CO-MISSING, CO-NOTIFY | No AML/CTF compliance officer, or AUSTRAC not notified within 14 days of the appointment | AUSTRAC reform guidance: AML/CTF compliance officer |
| GOVERNING-BODY-REPORT | The compliance officer has not reported to the governing body in 12 months | AUSTRAC reform guidance: AML/CTF compliance officer |
| PROGRAMME-REVIEW | AML/CTF policies not reviewed in 3 years | AUSTRAC reform guidance, Step 4 |
| RISK-ASSESSMENT | No ML/TF risk assessment on file, or not reviewed in 12 months (*your setting*) | AUSTRAC reform guidance, Step 4 |
| INDEPENDENT-EVALUATION | No independent evaluation in 3 years | AUSTRAC reform guidance, Step 5 (transitional rules stagger the first one) |
| ANNUAL-REPORT | The compliance report for the year to 30 June not submitted by 30 September | s47(2); AML/CTF Rules s9-9; AUSTRAC, annual compliance reports |
| VETTING, TRAINING | Personnel with AML duties without due diligence or yearly training (*your setting*) | AML/CTF Act 2006 as amended 2024 |
| SANCTIONS | A live customer with no sanctions screen on file | Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011; Charter of the United Nations Act 1945 |

Records are kept for 7 years (Part 10). Tipping off is prohibited (s123).
