# Research: what firms pay for AML software, and why this exists

Checked 2026-10-23. Prices as published, before GST.

## First AML

- Plans: Launch, Optimise and Transform, each "POA" (price on application). Launch is for firms "onboarding minimum 400 individuals or 100 complex entities / year". Source: https://www.firstaml.com/au/plans-and-pricing/
- How a contract is built, from First AML's help centre: an annual platform fee, included units, fixed units, pay-as-you-go variable units with an overage rate, and a one-off implementation fee. Source: https://help.firstaml.com/support/solutions/articles/69000880830-customising-disbursements
- Product: case management, KYC and KYB, structure mapping, biometric ID checks, PEP and sanctions screening, ongoing CDD, regulatory reporting. Sold to law and conveyancing, accounting, real estate and finance in Australia, New Zealand and the UK. Source: https://www.firstaml.com/

## Published Australian prices

- easyAML: Starter $179 a month, Professional $449 a month, Enterprise $999 a month, all plus GST on a 12-month subscription; KYC checks $15 to $20 and KYB checks $35 to $40 on top. Source: https://easyaml.com/pricing
- EngageAML (ChangeGPS): Lite from $233 a month, Pro from $667 a month, for 12 months, excluding GST; a $1,000 validation credit bundle required with Pro; KYC checks $7 to $12, KYB checks $20 to $55. Source: https://www.changegps.com.au/pricing/engageaml/

## Why now

- Australia: Tranche 2 businesses (lawyers, accountants, conveyancers, real estate, trust and company service providers) carry AML/CTF obligations from 1 July 2026: enrol, run a program, appoint and notify a compliance officer, do CDD, report, keep records.
- New Zealand: DIA became the single AML/CFT supervisor on 1 July 2026. The Statutes Amendment Act 2025 gave law firms 5 working days for a SAR and moved the PTR deadline to 20 working days.

## What the money buys

Most of the subscription is the case screens and the reminders. The checks themselves (document verification, PEP and sanctions lists, company registers) are a per-check cost that stays with a provider either way. This repo replaces the register, the rules and the paperwork, and records the check results.
