# training-due

People with AML duties whose training is missing, overdue or due in 30 days.

Read fresh data. Never invent a customer, a check result, a date or command output. These records hold personal information and must stay inside the firm.

```bash
node scripts/aml.mjs training-due --json
```

Replace angle-bracket placeholders with what the person told you; leave out optional flags they did not ask for. If a name or reference is ambiguous, list the candidates and ask. Group by firm. Nothing here runs a check, sends an email or lodges a report.
